This project provides a comprehensive analysis of Superstore sales performance, customers, orders, products, and geographic distribution.

The analysis transforms transactional retail data into meaningful business insights that help monitor sales performance, identify top customers and products, analyze order behavior, and understand sales distribution across different regions, states, and cities.

## Business Problem

Retail businesses generate large volumes of sales and transaction data. Without proper analysis, it can be difficult to understand sales trends, identify high-value customers, evaluate product performance, and determine which geographic markets contribute most to revenue.

This project provides a centralized analytical view of sales, customers, orders, products, and geography to support data-driven business decisions.

## Key Calculations

### Total Sales

Total sales are calculated from the transaction-level sales values.

### Sales Year-over-Year

Year-over-Year sales growth is calculated by comparing the current year's sales with the previous year's sales.

`Sales YoY % = (Current Year Sales - Previous Year Sales) ÷ Previous Year Sales`

### Customer Sales

Customer sales are aggregated to identify high-value customers and their contribution to overall revenue.

### Product Sales

Product sales are aggregated to identify the highest-performing products.

## Key Insights

* Sales performance varies across months and years, allowing seasonal and year-over-year trends to be identified.
* A relatively small group of products contributes a significant portion of total sales.
* Customer sales are concentrated among a number of high-value customers.
* Sales and customer distribution vary across geographic regions.
* Different product categories generate different levels of sales and order volume.
* Order volume and quantity provide additional context for understanding sales performance.
* Geographic analysis highlights states and cities with stronger sales contributions.
* Year-over-Year analysis helps identify changes in overall business performance.
